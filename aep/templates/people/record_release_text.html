AUTHORIZATION FOR RELEASE OF STUDENT RECORDS

Under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), the Delgado Adult Education Program will not release a student's education records to any person or organization outside the program without written consent from the student, or from a parent or legal guardian if the student is under 18. This form provides that consent.

By completing this form, I authorize the Delgado Adult Education Program to release the education records indicated below to the person or organization named below. Records may include enrollment and attendance history, assessment and test scores, progress and completion information, and other information in the student's file that is described on this form.

I understand that:

  • This authorization is voluntary, and the program will not condition enrollment or services on signing it.
  • Only the records described on this form will be released, and only to the person or organization named.
  • This authorization remains in effect until the end of the current program year. I may revoke it at any time by giving written notice to the Adult Education Program office; revocation does not affect records already released.
  • A copy of this authorization will be kept in the student's administrative folder, and I may request a copy of any record released under it.

Typing your name at the bottom of this form signifies an electronic signature. If the student is under 18, a parent or legal guardian must also sign.
